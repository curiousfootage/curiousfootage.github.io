Curious Footage

Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers Curious Footage Uploader, the private application that publishes videos to the Curious Footage YouTube channel.

Who this applies to

The application has a single user: the operator of the Curious Footage channel. It is not offered to the public and no third party can sign in to it.

What data is accessed

The application requests one Google permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload a video to the channel of the signed-in account.

This permission is used for one purpose only: uploading videos produced by the project to that same channel. It is never used to read, modify or delete anything else.

What data is stored

An OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in the project's private source-code repository. It is used solely to obtain a short-lived access token at upload time. No Google account data — no name, no email address, no contact list, no viewing history — is read, stored, logged or transmitted anywhere.

What is shared with third parties

Nothing. No data is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party. There is no analytics, no advertising network and no tracking of any kind on this site or in the application.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The refresh token is kept only while the project is active and is revoked immediately if the project stops. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
