Curious Footage

Short clips from the world's public film archives.

Curious Footage is a personal, non-commercial project that re-publishes short excerpts of public-domain archive film as vertical short-form video. Every clip is sourced from a verified public archive — Universal Newsreels, Prelinger Archives, FedFlix and NASA, all hosted by the Internet Archive — then re-cut, captioned in English and credited to its original source.

The uploader application

“Curious Footage Uploader” is the private software behind the channel. It runs on a schedule, assembles one short video per day and uploads it to the single YouTube channel owned by the project. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it has no public users.

Rights

Only works in the public domain, or under a licence that explicitly permits commercial reuse, are used. Every published clip is logged with its source, its identifier and its licence in a public rights ledger kept in the project repository.
