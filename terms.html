Scope

Curious Footage Uploader is private software used to publish videos to a single YouTube channel — the one it belongs to. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and no third party may use it.

Archive material

The videos published by this project are built from film held in public archives and believed to be in the public domain, or available under a licence permitting commercial reuse. Each published video credits its source in its description, and the project keeps a public ledger recording the source, identifier and licence of every clip used.

Rights concerns

If you hold rights in material you believe has been used in error, write to curiousfootagecontact@gmail.com. The clip will be removed on request, without discussion, and its source excluded from future use.
